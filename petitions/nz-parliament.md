# New Zealand Parliament e-petition

The wording to enter on Parliament's "Start a petition" form
(petitions.parliament.nz). Keep it identical on any paper sheets.

## Form fields

**Petition of:** The Asra Project (choose "on behalf of this organisation")

**Title** (72 of 80 characters)

> Uphold Israel's court order: let the Red Cross see Palestinian detainees

**Request** (273 of 300 characters, must start "That the House of Representatives")

> That the House of Representatives urge the Government to call publicly, with partner countries, on Israel to obey its own Supreme Court and let the Red Cross, Red Crescent and other impartial humanitarian agencies visit every Palestinian detainee, regularly and in private.

**Reason** (486 of 500 characters, optional)

> For three years the Red Cross has been barred from every Palestinian detainee. Israel holds over 9,200 of them, more than 3,000 without charge or trial. In June 2026 Israel's own Supreme Court ruled the ban unlawful and ordered visits to resume. They still have not. Red Cross visits are a basic protection under the Geneva Conventions, which bind New Zealand. New Zealand already acts with Australia, Canada, the UK and Norway on Israel. It can lead again, and the House can ask it to.

**Closing date:** 28 February 2027 (suggested; up to one year allowed, and the
Petitions team can change it later).

## Steps

1. Email the three fields to parliamentary.petitions@parliament.govt.nz for a
   wording check. Nothing can be changed after submitting.
2. Fill in the form, preview, then "Submit & verify email". Click the link in
   the email within 72 hours.
3. The Office of the Clerk moderates it and publishes it. Signing opens then.
4. Signers give first name, last name and email on Parliament's site only.
   Signatures from other websites, including ours, don't count.
5. Once the new House sits, an MP who has not signed presents it, within six
   months of closing. It then goes to the Petitions Committee.

## Sources for every figure

- 9,200+ held, more than 3,000 (3,101) in administrative detention without charge or trial: [HaMoked, October 2026](https://hamoked.org/prisoners-charts.php)
- Red Cross barred since October 2023; June 2026 ruling ordering visits to resume: [Al Jazeera, 4 June 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/4/israel-must-allow-icrc-to-visit-palestinians-in-prison-supreme-court-rules)
- What visits do (check treatment, carry messages to families): [ICRC, What we do for detainees](https://www.icrc.org/en/document/what-we-do-detainees) and [ICRC FAQ on Palestinian detainees](https://www.icrc.org/en/article/FAQ-icrc-and-palestinian-detainees)
- Visits not resumed: [Times of Israel, 28 September 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-gives-government-2-weeks-to-specify-terms-for-red-cross-visits-to-detainees/)
- New Zealand acting with Australia, Canada, the UK and Norway: [MFAT](https://www.mfat.govt.nz/en/countries-and-regions/middle-east/israel-hamas-conflict)

Re-check the facts after Israel's 11 October 2026 court deadline before submitting.
